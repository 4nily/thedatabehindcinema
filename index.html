Vol. I — Issue 01

THE DATA
BEHIND
CINEMA


By Janen.

01

Cinema

Movies have been around for a long time. Over the years, people started tracking statistics regarding cinema, such as how long they are, how much they cost, and how many people go to watch them in the cinema. Looking at that information and graphs provided later on demonstrates some patterns in how cinema has significantly changed.

As shown by the blue bars in the graph above, ticket prices have gradually increased over time. However, when adjusting for inflation represented by the red line, tickets back then were actually more expensive than one might expect, especially from the 1970s and 80s. So while prices may appear they’ve gone up a lot, the real increase isn’t as significant.

02

History

Cinema has changed a lot since movies first became popular. From silent films and black-and-white movies to modern digital filmmaking, new technology and changes in audience interests have continued to shape what movies look like today. Looking at different periods helps show how much the movie industry has changed over time.

03

Ticket Prices

Movie tickets have become more expensive over the years, but the increase looks different when inflation is taken into account. Comparing ticket prices across different decades gives a better idea of how much going to the movies actually cost people at the time.

04

Runtime

Back in the 1930s and 40s, movies were a bit shorter than they are today. After around the 1960s, most films settled into a range of about 100 to 110 minutes, and that hasn’t really changed much since.

The average movie length today, as of 2026, is about 114 minutes long.

05

Budgets

Movies can cost anywhere from a few thousand dollars to hundreds of millions of dollars to make. Looking at movie budgets can show how much studios are willing to spend and whether spending more money actually leads to a more successful movie.

06

Box Office

Box office revenue has increased over many decades due to an increase in ticket prices. A drop in 2020 is discernible as it was a time when theaters shut down due to the COVID-19 pandemic. Since then, revenue has recovered, but not entirely as it was before.

07

Ratings

Movie ratings give another way to look at how people respond to films. Comparing ratings from audiences and critics can show how opinions about the same movie can sometimes be very different.

08

Attendance

Ticket sales were much higher early on, then slowly declined over time with the rise of TV and later streaming entertainment options such as Netflix, Disney+, Paramount, Sony, Max, Hulu, Amazon Prime Video, Apple TV+, etc. A big drop in 2020 is discernible as theaters closed during the COVID-19 pandemic. After the pandemic, ticket sales somewhat recovered, but they still haven’t fully returned to earlier levels.

09

Actors & Directors

Actors and directors can have a major effect on a movie's success. Some become connected to many popular movies, while others become known for a particular style or type of film. Looking at their careers can show how much individual people contribute to cinema.

10

Movie Theaters

Movie theaters have changed quite a bit over the years. Single-screen theaters have been replaced by larger multiplexes, while features such as IMAX, premium seating, and better sound systems have become more common. These changes show how theaters have adapted to keep attracting audiences.

11

Streaming

Streaming has changed the way people watch movies. Services such as Netflix, Disney+, and other platforms allow people to watch thousands of movies from home. Looking at streaming data can show how this has affected movie theaters and traditional movie releases.

12

Genre

Drama is the most common genre by a wide margin. Comedy comes next, while genres like documentary, sci-fi, and animation make up smaller portions overall. In general, most movies tend to fall into either the drama or the comedy category.

Letterboxd started as a film-tracking site and has grown into a larger platform. In 2016, people logged around 20 million film ratings, and by 2025 that number had risen to around 700 million. This difference underscores how watching movies has become an activity people actively log and review.

13

Technology

Technology has played a major role in changing movies. Sound, color, digital cameras, CGI, 3D, and other developments have changed both how movies are made and what audiences expect to see on screen.

14

Movies Around the World

Movies are made and watched all over the world, not just in Hollywood. Comparing different countries can show which film industries are the largest, which movies are most popular, and how different countries have developed their own styles of filmmaking.

15

The Future of Cinema

Cinema is still changing, and it is difficult to know exactly what it will look like in the future. Streaming, new technology, changing movie theaters, and artificial intelligence could all affect how movies are made, released, and watched.
